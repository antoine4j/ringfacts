RingFacts

A news pipeline that watches a few people in the news and delivers only the new facts about them. It collects every article it can find, classifies each one, extracts its key fact, counts a story reported by nine outlets once, and grades its importance: career news is posted at once, lesser updates go into a weekly digest, and noise is held back. Today it watches three MMA fighters and posts to a Telegram chat.
