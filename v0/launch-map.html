The launch

The friends' group joins after a four-week window of v0 posting to the test chat, with a reaction to every tier-1 post and digest. It passes when the last two weeks show:

  • no career event missed, checked against what actually happened;
  • no repeat noticed in tier 1 (a split inside a digest does not count);
  • at most 1 in 10 posts or digests with a 👎.
