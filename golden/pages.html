How these pages work

There are two kinds.

Read-only pagesThe Answer Key Check
WhichPlan, Claim Map, Report, Questionsthe only interactive one
What clicking doesFilters, pickers and the theme switch change your view only. Nothing is saved, and I can't see it.Every correction, note and "checked" is saved the moment you make it.
Where it is savedNowhere. The page is a snapshot of files in the repo.In the page's own small database on claude.ai. It's not on your laptop and not in the repo. It survives when I republish the page, and only you can open it.
How I read itI don't need to. I build these pages from the repo.With a tool that reads the page's database directly (ArtifactData). No copy and paste. I then write your corrections into the repo as your labels.
How it changesI edit the source file or rerun its builder, then republish to the same link.The same way, and your saved answers stay.

Any page also takes comments through claude.ai's comment button. I can read them when you ask me to.

Why pages instead of HTML files

  • One link that stays the same. A new version replaces the old one at the same address, and claude.ai keeps the earlier versions.
  • Works on your phone. A local HTML file opens only on the laptop that has it.
  • Your input comes back to me. On a local file, anything you click is lost unless you paste it into the chat. A page with a database hands it to me directly.
  • Nothing depends on the page alone. Every page is built from files in the repo, which stays the record. If a page disappeared, I could rebuild it.

So for anything you'll look at, or give answers on, a page is better for both of us. When you want the file itself, for instance to keep a copy, ask for that.
