Orange line in a cell: how many of its claims carry a doubt. A doubt is one of: the three readers split (the classifier asked three times, options in three orders, not all giving the same answer); the claim's articles land in different cells of this view; classifier and extractor disagree on "not about him" or on whether he himself is speaking; in v3 also: the classifier says no option fits well, or its source and act contradict each other; in v5 and v6 (one pass, so no reader split): source and act contradict, the fact answer and its yes/no question disagree, source is "himself" but "he speaks" says no, or source, act or fact is under 40% confidence; in v7 also: on the training set, an answer differs from your key. Hatched cells are impossible by definition.
