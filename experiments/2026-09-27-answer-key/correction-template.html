How this page works, and where to start

The answer key is 59 of the 300 articles, picked to cover every answer value and the known traps. The classifier questions were tuned on 39 of them ("key · tune") and checked on the other 20 ("key · held back"), which were never looked at while changing questions. Every score in the report is measured on these 59, so they matter most.

The other 241 were labelled the same way afterwards and never used for tuning. They become the rest of the golden labels.

How an answer was reached: two Fable readers labelled each article separately and blind. "Both readers" means they agreed. Where they differed, an Opus reader answered too: "2 of 3 readers" means two matched. "Readers split" means all three gave different answers, so there is no answer until you choose one.

"Classifier v6" beside an answer means the classifier disagrees with the readers there: one of them is wrong, so those are the answers worth reading.

You don't need to read all 300. Work down the numbered filters:

  1. Key articles where the readers differed: settle these first.
  2. The rest of the key: read the answers the classifier disagrees with.
  3. Other articles where the readers differed.
  4. Other articles where only the classifier differs: read just the flagged answers.
  5. Where the readers and the classifier all agree: check the 15-article sample. If it is clean, the rest of that group can stand.
